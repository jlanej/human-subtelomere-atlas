Structural alleles

One row per structural allele: the representative end's core blocks (≥ 10 kb) from the telomere boundary (left, dark bar) inward. Home blocks carry the arm's own colour; other blocks are coloured by their best single CHM13 v2.0 end; members of a paralog family share a hue and differ in lightness; dashed outline = block labelled by paralog family. Triangles mark median core switch positions; the thin bar under a row spans the members' minimum to maximum switch. Hover over a row, or focus it with the keyboard, for details (structure, families, best ends, nativity class, frequencies, evidence); click or press Enter to filter the member table to that allele.

Labels are relative to CHM13: a colour change is a change in which CHM13 end is most similar, not necessarily a structural breakpoint. Palette: methods.
