Evidence tiers

Every structural allele and haplogroup carries an evidence tier (from SCHEMA.md):

Tiers. Evidence lines: R = present in >= 3 unrelated samples (no parent-child pairs exist among Release 2 samples in samples.tsv); orthogonal O = verkko (a member's Verkko assembly agrees), reads (HG002 Q100 reads cross the member's switch, or the hifiasm read layout supports the member's end), pedigree (intact transmission with parent and child carrying the allele), t2t_assembly (a member in HG002 Q100, or in CHM13 for non-native alleles and haplogroups). A = R and >= 1 O, or >= 2 O. B = R without O, or 2 samples with >= 1 O. C = all others (singleton marks one-sample alleles).

9. Track-based mosaic: paralog families and boundary-shift stability

Method (atlas/subtel_atlas/tracks.py): one alignment pass per end (subtelomere vs the 48 CHM13 500 kb ends, minimap2 -x asm20 -c --cs=short --secondary=yes -N 200 -p 0.01); per-base match tracks per CHM13 end; sliding 5 kb identity on a 100 bp grid anchored at the contig tip (independent of where the boundary is called); grid labels = home if a home end is within 0.5 percentage points of the best identity, else the paralog family of the best end, none below 0.8; runs < 2 kb merged; boundaries refined at base level on the label tracks. S5/S10/S20 states are the majority grid label of the window.

0. One implementation for catalogue and tool (pipeline_equivalence.tsv)

The package contains every method used by the catalogue build:

  • the telomere caller and window rule;
  • the identity-track mosaic and paralog families (tracks.py, imported by pipeline steps 04t, 06t and 08e);
  • the structure and allele-matching rules (alleles.py, step 08);
  • the arm rules (arms.py, step 05);
  • the haplogroup sketch and alignment (haplo.py, sketch.py, step 07).

tests/test_query_tool.py::test_pipeline_imports_shared_code checks that the pipeline imports them.

Equivalence is shown end to end. The tool runs its own minimap2 alignment and reproduces the catalogue tables in sections (a) and (c). The byte-level checks of the original refactoring, made on the bin-based first build, are listed as historical rows.

Review of the revision-2 changes made to the package by the catalogue build (tracks.py; edits to query.py, refs.py, alleles.py):

  • The track method and allele tie-break are consistent with the pipeline.
  • Three gaps were fixed in query.py:
    1. The catalogue's window extension for boundaries beyond 20 kb was missing.
    2. Track queries are now named <end_id>@0, as in step 04t, because minimap2 breaks score ties with the query name.
    3. Ends without alignments now get a mosaic, as in step 06t.
  • Added per-end outputs: the ±500 bp boundary-stability calls (same computation as step 08e), window_extended, sa_is_native and sa_nativity_class.

(b) Leave-one-sample-out (loso*.tsv)

For each of the 60 samples (5,293 Release 2 ends), the sample's ends were removed from the references before assignment.

  • Structural alleles. Re-clustered from the remaining defining ends with the catalogue rule (complete linkage, 10 kb Chebyshev cut); each cluster keeps the ID of its majority original allele.
  • Haplogroups. A medoid from the held-out sample was replaced by the remaining clustered member with the highest mean ANI. Haplogroups left with fewer than 3 samples were dropped from the representatives. Haplogroups were not re-clustered (see Deviations).

(e) mappy backend (backend_mappy*.tsv)

On 400 ends, mappy (all chains kept, -P mode; mappy cannot set -p 0.01) agrees with the minimap2 backend for:

  • S5 86.5%, S10 89.8% and S20 93.0%;
  • profile 60.3%, core structure 92.0% and structural allele 91.5%;
  • haplogroup 100%.

It runs at 3.9 s per end single-threaded. On 80 of these ends, mappy's default chain filtering did worse (structural allele 77.5% vs 88.7%). Under revision 2 the mappy fallback is therefore a clearly weaker approximation than in revision 1. The CLI prints a warning when it is used, and the minimap2 executable is required for catalogue-exact results.

Failure modes

  1. Ambiguous structural alleles. An end can lie within 10 kb of two same-structure allele medians. The query rule takes the nearest median, then the larger allele; the catalogue's clustering can place a defining end in the other allele (11/5,981). Flag: sa_multiple_candidates; read sa_candidates.
  2. Boundary sensitivity of non-native calls. Moving the boundary by 500 bp changes 5-6% of foreign and within-family allele calls and fewer than 0.5% of native ones. Flag: boundary_unstable (81% sensitivity, no false alarms; 6 of the 574 ends it calls stable changed under re-aligned shifts).
  3. Diffuse haplogroups. Medoid assignment differs from UPGMA membership mostly in acroP, 10q and PAR1. Flags: hg_close_second, low hg_ani.
  4. Unrepresented rare haplogroups (1-2 samples) have no medoid in representatives.fa.gz. Their ends are reported below threshold (170/189) or absorbed into a neighbour (19/189).
  5. mappy fallback. About 92% agreement on structural alleles; use the minimap2 executable.
  6. Arm inference without names or probes (mosaic rule): 84% correct at group level. Contig-name arms assume standard orientation.
  7. Telomeres < 500 bp are not reported in contig mode, by definition; --min-telomere lowers the threshold.

Deviations and scope

  • Self-consistency used a stratified sample of 5,981 of the 20,284 non-pedigree ends (29%), not the whole catalogue.
  • Leave-one-sample-out re-clustered structural alleles exactly. For haplogroups it re-selected medoids and representation without re-running UPGMA.
  • The boundary-shift test used 600 ends (half with non-native alleles, i.e. non-native ends over-represented relative to the catalogue).
  • The probe-rule table was carried over from the revision-1 run, because the arm method and arm calls did not change.
  • The per-end nativity_class field was added to the tool after the self-consistency query had started. Its agreement (5,981/5,981) was computed from the recorded core structures with the same function.
  • No HG002 Release 2 comparison (no such ends in v0.1). Read mode was tested on 719 HG002 reads from two technologies only.
  • The test suite uses reduced references (6 CHM13 ends, telomeric 150 kb kept, rest N-masked). Its golden outputs test regression and determinism; agreement with the catalogue is covered here and by the optional full-reference test.

10. Paralog families, the home rule, nativity classes and none blocks

Plain-language definitions. At every 100 bp point of an end's subtelomere, the identity of the surrounding 5 kb to each of the 48 CHM13 v2.0 ends is read from one alignment of the whole window. (1) Home rule: the point is labelled home if a CHM13 copy of the end's own arm (or of an arm of its pooled group: acroP, PAR1, PAR2) is within 0.5 percentage points of the best identity; home therefore wins ties. (2) Paralog families: some CHM13 ends share long, near-identical duplicated blocks, so the best end at a point can flip between them with small sequence or boundary changes. Two CHM13 ends are put in one family when, over all defining catalogue ends, they are tied with each other (both within the margin of the best) at >= 0.75 of the points where the less often tied of the two is tied (and at >= 200 points); families are the connected groups of such pairs (paralog_families.tsv). A point that is not home is labelled with the family of its best end (PF01...), or with that end's arm if it belongs to no family. The family is a property of the CHM13 ends across the catalogue, not of the individual block: inside a given block the other members may or may not be tied (column mean_tied of mosaic_blocks.tsv; the single best CHM13 end of each block is kept in best_end and of each fixed window in S*_best_end). (3) none: the best identity to every CHM13 end is < 0.80 at that point, i.e. sequence without a close CHM13 counterpart (for example sequence absent from CHM13, or diverged beyond 20%); it is neither home nor foreign evidence. After merging blocks < 10 kb into their neighbours, a none block in a core structure is >= 10 kb of such sequence; switches next to a none block are kept at grid resolution.

Worked example: HG02129#2#CM085868.1#R (catalogue arm 20q, allele SA-20q-01; one of the query-tool test windows in tests/fixtures/windows.fa.gz). Its outer 31,040 bp of subtelomere best match CHM13 3q. In the previous build its form was 20q>3q; it is now 20q>PF04 (core structure PF04|home), because CHM13 3q and 19p form family PF04 (co-tied at 0.81 of the points where the less often tied of the two is tied, over the catalogue). S20_best_end keeps chr3q. Over this block the end matches CHM13 3q at a median 99.92%, 19p at 98.34% and its own arm 20q at 98.36% (19p within the 0.5-point margin of 3q at 0% of 311 points): the block is specifically 3q-like, and the family label records only that 3q belongs to a pair of CHM13 ends that are often interchangeable elsewhere in the catalogue.
