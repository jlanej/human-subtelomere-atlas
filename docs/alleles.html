Alleles

All 647 structural alleles and 593 sequence haplogroups of catalogue v0.1. Search matches every word against ID, arm, structure, form, family and evidence (for example 20q PF04, 16q|home or pedigree). Click an ID to open its arm page. Frequencies are among Release 2 ends of the arm in each superpopulation. Structures read telomere → centromere: home = own arm, PFnn = paralog family, an arm name = that CHM13 arm, none = no CHM13 end within 0.80 identity (definitions).

Search index: data/alleles_index.json (columns id, type, arm, structure, form, nativity_class, families, tier, evidence, n_ends, n_samples, r2_freq, AFR, AMR, EAS, EUR, SAS). Nativity and family filters apply to structural alleles; haplogroups are sequence clusters and have no structure of their own (their main structural alleles are listed).
