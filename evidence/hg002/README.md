# HG002: multi-platform spanning reads and trio transmission (criteria v1)

Read-level test of every non-native HG002 Q100 v1.1 end of the catalogue (24 claims: 20 foreign, 4 within-family; the same 24 as
the calibration, since `ends.tsv` contains no other non-native HG002 end). The criteria are the frozen v1 criteria
([../CRITERIA.md](../CRITERIA.md), [../criteria_v1.json](../criteria_v1.json), sha `a89c5eaf023ed032`). The decision logic of
`atlas/subtel_atlas/readevidence.py` was not changed. The scripts here only choose which reads enter, how datasets are grouped and
how the tables are written.

* [claims.tsv](claims.tsv): one row per claim. Columns:
  * end, haplotype, structural allele, structure, donor D / proximal comparator P / recipient R;
  * assembly switch, its CHM13 projection (`chm13_p_switch_tip` = distance from the CHM13 telomere end of P;
    `chm13_p_switch_coord` = 1-based coordinate on the CHM13 v2.0 chromosome of P), and the assembly projection of the switch;
  * pooled counts and the verdict, with the reason when the claim is not confirmed;
  * per dataset `counts_<dataset>` = eligible/conclusive/conclusive switch-concordant/contradicting;
  * the HERRO-only verdict, the calibration-scope verdict, and strictly native control counts.
* [claims_by_dataset.tsv](claims_by_dataset.tsv): the same counts and a verdict per claim and dataset.
* [reads.tsv](reads.tsv): every conclusive or contradicting read (1,237 rows; carrier and HG002 other-haplotype roles). Columns:
  * read id, molecule key, `duplicate_of`, dataset, source URL, region queried, run (PacBio movie; ONT records carry no read group);
  * read length, orientation, telomere bp and offset;
  * donor-segment units/purity, log10 LRs, switch position in the read (`switch_read`, 95% interval `switch_ci_read_*`), in
    subtelomere coordinates, in the tip frame of CHM13 P and as CHM13 v2.0 chromosome coordinates of P (`switch_ci_P_chm13_*`);
  * anchor chromosome, tip coordinates, MAPQ, length, unique bp and tier; integrity fields;
  * the HG002 Q100 alignment (contig, position, MAPQ, SA tag, query coverage, `assembly_concordant` = contiguous on the claimed
    contig end); the verdict.
* [controls_by_dataset.tsv](controls_by_dataset.tsv): HG002 other-haplotype reads by role and dataset.
* [fetch_manifest.tsv](fetch_manifest.tsv): 448 slices (16 alignment files x 28 contig ends). Each row gives the URL, region,
  record count and SHA-256 of the slice.
* [trio_claims.tsv](trio_claims.tsv), [trio_reads.tsv](trio_reads.tsv), [trio_fetch_manifest.tsv](trio_fetch_manifest.tsv):
  the same for HG003 and HG004.
* Evidence BAMs (git-ignored, regenerated by the commands below). Each read group names the dataset and has `DS` = platform and
  source URL. Tags: `XC` claim(s), `XV` verdict(s), `XR` role(s), `XK` switch concordance.
  * `work/evidence/hg002/evidence_bam/hg002_evidence.chm13v2.bam`: CHM13 v2.0 alignments (whole read, tip frame, minimap2
    map-ont) of the 954 conclusive or contradicting HG002 reads; 15 read groups; 46 MB.
  * `work/evidence/hg002/evidence_bam/hg002_evidence.hg002q100.bam`: the original primary alignments of the same reads to the
    HG002 Q100 v1.1 contig ends (MM/ML base-modification tags removed); 98 MB.
  * `work/evidence/trio/evidence_bam/trio_evidence.chm13v2.bam`: CHM13 v2.0 alignments of the 333 conclusive or contradicting
    parent reads.

## 1. HG002: all public Q100 long-read alignments

**Datasets.** All long-read alignments to HG002 Q100 v1.1 in the public `mapping/` folder of the polishing project
(`https://human-pangenomics.s3.amazonaws.com/T2T/HG002/assemblies/polishing/HG002/v1.1/mapping/`) were used. This is 16 files:
* the 11 of `sets.tsv`;
* five further long-read sets: ONT LC24 ultra-long (`ont_lc24_ulk`), ONT LC24 (`ont_lc24_apk`), ONT Q28 LC24 (`ont_q28_lc24`), ONT
  R9.4.1 (`ont_methylation_recall_oct2024`) and PacBio HiFi DeepConsensus v1.1 (`hifi_dcv1.1`).

Short-read alignments (Illumina, Element, Onso, Ultima) cannot satisfy the telomere-plus-anchor criteria and were not used.

**Read capture.** For each dataset and contig end, the query takes primary alignments over the outer
`max(5 kb, s0 + min(switch, 100 kb) + 30 kb)` of the contig end. Here s0 is the telomere plus junk length of the end window,
and the switch is the largest catalogue switch at the arm. Two read classes are evaluated:
* `end`: reads whose primary alignment overlaps the outer 5 kb. This is the calibration population; all reads of at least
  5 kb are evaluated.
* `proximal`: telomere-reaching reads whose primary alignment lies further in. They enter only if the module's caller finds a
  terminal telomere.

Only 8 `proximal` reads qualified, all non-informative. Every telomere-reaching read that crosses a switch therefore aligns
across the contig end in the Q100 alignments.

**Independence.** Molecules were de-duplicated across datasets per claim, by ONT read UUID (both UUIDs of a duplex read) or
PacBio movie/ZMW. Three files are re-packagings of reads already in other files, with every evaluated carrier read a duplicate:
* `hifi_pb_sprq` = `hifi_revio_3cells` + `hifi_sprq_30hr`;
* `hifi_revio_30hr` is a subset of `hifi_revio_3cells`;
* `ont_q28_lc24` = `ont_lc24_ulk` + `ont_q28`.

2,241 duplicate carrier rows were removed, leaving 7,063 unique carrier molecules. The 12 remaining primary datasets have
disjoint molecules. ONT HERRO-corrected reads are derived: 449 of 478 share their UUID with `ont_q28` reads. They are evaluated
and reported separately (`herro_*` columns) and never counted.

**Reproduction of the calibration.** The calibration scope (`ont_ul_dorado`, `ont_duplex`, `hifi_revio_3cells`; `end` reads)
reproduces the calibration exactly: 24 of 24 claim verdicts, and identical conclusive switch-concordant counts per claim
(`calibration_scope_verdict`; [../calibration/claims_calibration.tsv](../calibration/claims_calibration.tsv)).

**Claims.** Pooled over the primary datasets (unique molecules):
* **15 of 24 confirmed**: the same 15 as in the calibration;
* **1 supported**: 9q maternal, with one conclusive 685 kb ONT LC24 ultra-long read plus 143 consistent reads;
* **8 not confirmed**;
* **none contradicted**.

Of the 15 confirmed claims:
* 13 are confirmed by conclusive switch-concordant reads from at least 2 independent datasets;
* 10 by at least 2 sequencing chemistries (ONT R10.4.1, ONT R9.4.1, PacBio HiFi);
* 2 (19q maternal and 9p maternal) by all three chemistries;
* 5 by ONT R10.4.1 reads only (15q paternal, 17p paternal, 6q paternal, 3q maternal, 8p maternal), and 2 of these from a single
  dataset (15q paternal with 2 reads, 6q paternal with 12, both `ont_ul_dorado`).

Across all claims there are 421 unique conclusive carrier reads:
* 395 are switch-concordant;
* 364 align contiguously, without a supplementary alignment, to the claimed Q100 contig end;
* 368 have an anchor of tier `unique`.

## 2. Trio: HG003 (father) and HG004 (mother)

**Sources (public, range-readable).** GIAB Ashkenazi trio, NIST RM8392, on the NCBI GIAB FTP
(`https://ftp-trace.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ReferenceSamples/giab/data/AshkenazimTrio/`), plus ONT open data:
* PacBio HiFi Revio (2023; 46x HG003, 36x HG004), pbmm2 to **CHM13 v2.0**;
* PacBio HiFi Sequel II, 15/20 kb chemistry 2, DeepVariant-haplotagged, to **CHM13 v2.0**;
* UCSC ultra-long ONT R9.4.1 (PromethION 2019, Guppy 3.2.5; Shafin et al. 2020, doi:10.1038/s41587-020-0503-6), to **GRCh38**;
* ONT R10.4.1 sup, GIAB 2023.05 release (3 flow cells HG003, 6 flow cells HG004;
  `https://ont-open-data.s3.amazonaws.com/giab_2023.05/analysis/`), to **GRCh38**. These are CRAMs, decoded with the GRCh38
  no-alt analysis set from NCBI.

No HG003 or HG004 long reads aligned to HG002 Q100 exist in the Q100 `mapping/` folder; the parental sets there are short reads.

**Method.** The public alignment's reference is used only to retrieve reads. The queries are:
* the recipient arm: outer 3 kb + min(switch, 100 kb) + 30 kb;
* the outer 5 kb of the donor and proximal ends, offset past the terminal N runs of GRCh38.

Reads ≥ 5 kb with a terminal telomere are evaluated against CHM13 v2.0 exactly as for HG002: 10,287 parent reads, HG003 5,706 and
HG004 4,581. Parents are unphased, so the unphased claim rule applies. Switch concordance is tested against the HG002 assembly
projection of the claimed, i.e. transmitted, haplotype. Every claim is tested in both parents. The transmitting parent is the
test (HG003 for PATERNAL, HG004 for MATERNAL); the other parent is reported only.

**Result.** Of the 15 claims confirmed in HG002, the transmitting parent confirms 4 and supports 1. The confirmed four:
* **9p maternal (15q-like).** HG004 has 13 conclusive concordant reads (R10.4.1 and R9.4.1) and no contradicting read. HG003 has
  no conclusive read and 17 contradicting reads, so the father is native over the donor interval. Transmission is maternal.
* **19q maternal (22q-like).** HG004 has 6 conclusive concordant reads with switches at 11.4-16.7 kb, plus 4 contradicting reads
  (conclusive fraction 0.60, as expected for a heterozygote). HG003 also carries a 22q-like 19q block, but its 6 conclusive reads
  place the switch at 5.5-9.2 kb, discordant with the transmitted haplotype. The transmitted switch is maternal.
* **17p maternal (11p-like).** HG004 has 5 conclusive concordant reads (switch 18.9-23.5 kb) and 12 contradicting reads.
* **17p paternal (11p-like).** HG003 has 4 conclusive concordant reads (switch 9.3-14.5 kb, concordant through the ±2 kb
  tolerance and the CHM13 projection).

17p is not haplotype-discriminating in this trio. HG003 also has two conclusive reads with the switch at 21-27 kb, which are
concordant with the maternal claim, so both parents carry 11p-like 17p blocks. 17p transmission is consistent with the claimed
parent but not uniquely assignable.

19p maternal is **supported** in HG004: one HiFi Sequel II read plus 20 consistent reads, and no conclusive read in HG003. The
other 19 transmitting-parent tests are not confirmed, and none is contradicted by the claim rule. The main cause is the anchor:
* parental reads are shorter than the HG002 ultra-long sets (HiFi N50 ~17 kb; R10.4.1 non-ultra-long);
* the 2019 R9.4.1 ultra-long reads reach the anchor only at some arms.

3q maternal shows two HG004 HiFi reads conclusive with a switch at ~11 kb, discordant with the transmitted 31-39 kb switch. This
is a different 11p-like 3q block in the mother, reported only.

## Limitations

* **Parental reads.** For the parents, retrieval via GRCh38 misses telomeric reads whose primary alignment lies elsewhere in
  GRCh38, and the R9.4.1 telomere is detected only with the C1 artefact motifs. Parental not-confirmed calls therefore mostly
  mean "not assessable with these reads", not absence.
* **Parental evidence BAMs.** Only CHM13 alignments are written for the parents; no own-assembly line exists for them.
* **Independence.** Independence is judged at the molecule level, by read UUID or ZMW. Different libraries from one DNA
  extraction are counted as independent datasets.
* **Controls.** Strictly native HG002 controls exist only at 16p, 17q and 4q. For the other arms the false-conclusive rate rests
  on the calibration's 1KG controls ([../calibration/CALIBRATION.md](../calibration/CALIBRATION.md)).
* **Out of scope for v1.** Claims whose switch lies beyond the window (16p paternal) or whose donor block is mosaic need a
  criteria revision with its own calibration; they are reported as not confirmed under v1.

## Reproduce

```
source work/config.local.sh   # ATLAS_INPUT_DIR: CHM13 v2.0 FASTA (+fai), 500 kb ends; local .bai copies (optional)
A=$ATLAS_INPUT_DIR
python evidence/scripts/hg002_multiplatform.py fetch --index-dir $A/hg002 -j 4
python evidence/scripts/hg002_multiplatform.py eval --chm13-ends $A/subtel/foreign/ref/chm13_ends500k.fa \
    --genome work/readevidence/chm13v2.map-ont.mmi -t 4
python evidence/scripts/hg002_multiplatform.py report --chm13-ends $A/subtel/foreign/ref/chm13_ends500k.fa \
    --chm13-fai $A/ref/chm13v2.0.fa.gz.fai
# GRCh38 no-alt analysis set (NCBI GCA_000001405.15) for the ONT R10.4.1 parental CRAMs
python evidence/scripts/trio_reads.py fetch --grch38 work/ref/GRCh38_no_alt.fna -j 3
python evidence/scripts/trio_reads.py eval --grch38 work/ref/GRCh38_no_alt.fna \
    --chm13-ends $A/subtel/foreign/ref/chm13_ends500k.fa --genome work/readevidence/chm13v2.map-ont.mmi -t 4
python evidence/scripts/trio_reads.py report --grch38 work/ref/GRCh38_no_alt.fna \
    --chm13-ends $A/subtel/foreign/ref/chm13_ends500k.fa --chm13-fai $A/ref/chm13v2.0.fa.gz.fai
```

The fetch takes about 10 min (1.1 GB of slices). On 4 threads, evaluation takes about 1 h 45 min, and genome-wide anchoring
(1.1 Gb of query sequence) about 35 min. The trio takes about 80 min.
